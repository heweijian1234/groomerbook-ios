## Notes 字段全文（英文，直接贴）

```
Thank you for your feedback. We have prepared the following information to support the review of GroomerBook (v1.0).

1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone (latest iOS) is attached to this App Review Information entry. It shows the complete user flow from cold launch: creating a client, adding the client's pet with grooming details, booking an appointment with a reminder, viewing the Home Screen visit card (navigate/call actions), and marking an appointment complete. The app has no account creation, no login, and no user-generated content sharing features, so no account deletion or content reporting flows are applicable.

2. APP PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
GroomerBook is a local record-keeping utility for independent and mobile pet groomers. It solves a real operational problem: groomers track client contact details, per-pet grooming recipes (blade/shampoo/style), bite-risk warnings, vaccine expiry dates, appointment scheduling with drive-time buffers, no-show history with deposit hints, and monthly income/mileage summaries — all of which independent groomers previously managed in paper notebooks or spreadsheets. Target audience: self-employed mobile pet groomers (solo operators, not salons).

3. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
No account or setup required — the app works immediately after install:
- Clients tab: tap + to add a client (name, phone, email, service address, family contact, preferences). Contacts can be imported from the device address book.
- Pets are added within the client flow (name, type: dog/cat/horse/other, breed, grooming recipe, temperament, bite-risk level, vaccine expiry, photo).
- Today tab: tap + to create an appointment (client, pets, date/time, duration, drive buffer, optional reminder 1-10 hours before). The visit card shows one-tap Navigate (Apple Maps) and Call actions.
- Appointments can be marked Done / No-show; no-show history automatically suggests deposits for repeat offenders.
- Me tab: feedback via email.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app has no network access, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no external services of any kind. All data is stored locally on the device. The Contacts and Photo integrations are iOS system frameworks invoked only by explicit user action.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions. All data is local and no region-dependent services or content exist.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. GroomerBook is a business record-keeping utility for pet grooming services. It is not a medical, veterinary, or regulated service, and it performs no notarization, diagnosis, or treatment functions. All content is original.

Demo account: Not applicable — the app requires no account or login.
```
