GroomerBook — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

The short version

GroomerBook does not collect any data. Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

How the app works

  • Your clients, pets, appointments and notes are stored only on your device
  • The app works fully offline; it never requests your location, contacts (unless you explicitly pick a contact to fill in a client card), photos (unless you explicitly attach one to a pet), or any other data
  • Contact and photo access happens only when you tap the corresponding button — nothing is read automatically

What we never do

  • We never upload, transmit, or sell anything
  • We never track you, profile you, or share data with third parties
  • There is nothing to leak: the app has no network access at all

Data removal

Deleting the app removes all of your records immediately and completely. There is no server copy, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.
